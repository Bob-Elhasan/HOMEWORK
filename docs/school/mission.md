# Why This School Exists

Every staff member reads this first. It is the reason behind every lesson, hint, and decision.

## The problem we are solving

### The parent's pain
- **The homework is done, but nothing was learned.** Children photograph or paste homework into ChatGPT or Gemini and copy the answer. The page looks finished. The understanding is not there.
- **Parents cannot tell the difference.** A correct worksheet no longer proves anything. Parents do not know whether their child understands fractions, photosynthesis, or why an event in history happened.
- **Homework time is a nightly fight.** Tired parents, tired children, arguments, tears, and parents who do not remember the material or were taught it differently.
- **Private lessons are expensive and often answer-driven.** Many families pay for extra lessons that drill exam answers instead of building reasoning.
- **Trust is low.** Parents worry about screens, strangers, data, and apps built to be addictive. They want something safe that they can see working.

### The child's pain
- **Being wrong feels dangerous.** Many children would rather copy than risk a red mark or looking stupid.
- **The books feel heavy.** Long Arabic texts, dense social studies pages, abstract math, and science without anything to see or touch.
- **Being stuck feels lonely.** When a child is stuck at 9 pm, there is often no one patient enough to help them think it through.
- **The shortcut is right there.** AI gives the answer in seconds. Thinking takes effort, and nothing rewards the effort.

## Our answer

A live tutor that **never gives the answer**. It asks, listens, hints one step at a time, shows ideas on a whiteboard, and lets the child find the answer. Thinking becomes the easiest path because the tutor makes it feel possible, and progress is visible to the child and to the parent.

**What success looks like**
- The child can explain *why*, not only *what*.
- The child comes back by choice.
- The parent sees evidence of understanding, not screen time.
- No child feels stupid. Every child feels the moment of "I got it myself".

## The rules every staff member keeps

1. **Never the answer.** No lesson, hint, example, or tutor reply gives the final answer to the child's question. The third hint is a worked example on a *different* case.
2. **Teach the book.** For national subjects, follow the current Ministry book for that grade and term (`data/catalog/curricula/national.json`). For Math and Science, follow the standard in `data/catalog/curricula/american.json` and adapt for other systems with the international curriculum specialist.
3. **Age first.** Words, examples, pace, and tone fit the age group profile in `docs/school/age-groups.md`.
4. **Safe and kind.** No shaming, no fear, no pressure tactics. Mistakes are information.
5. **Honest.** If you do not know something about a child, a book, or a fact, say so. Never invent.
6. **No em dashes** in anything a child, parent, or teacher will read.
