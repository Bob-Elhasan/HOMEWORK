# Council: Mascot, fennec fox or abstract character, and its name
Date: 2026-11-26    Chair: principal
Members: course-designer-chief, course-designer-ethics, brand-illustration-designer, child-development-specialist, grade-head-g2, grade-head-g8, course-designer-bilingual, product-manager (guest)

Note: the council members are AI staff personas. No real children were tested and no trademark search was run.

## Question
Should HOMEWORK's tutor mascot be a fennec fox or a more abstract brand-born character (like Duolingo's Duo)? Which name: Homie, Eko, Kiro, or Nudj?

Context: voice-first tutor for grades 2 to 8 in Egypt that gives hints, never answers. Current docs call the tutor "Ibi" with an age-banded family, including an older sibling who "looks about 17". The founder likes the fennec (big ears mean listening) and favours Homie. Constraint: the character must never pretend to be a person or a real child (ethics H2) and must not manipulate.

## Positions (round 1)
- **course-designer-chief:** abstract creature with fennec-like ears as a cue. It can mature over seven years; older pupils drop cute animals before 14. Name Eko.
- **course-designer-ethics:** stylised fennec is fine and safer than the human-looking "about 17" sibling. One non-human species for all bands. Eko. Against Homie (claims friendship) and Nudj (reads as manipulation).
- **brand-illustration-designer:** stylised fennec, simple shapes, big ears, no clothes or school uniform. Eko. Risk: the fox as trickster in folk tales (Kalila wa Dimna); no sly looks on the expression sheet.
- **child-development-specialist:** stylised fennec, Eko. Honest lines matter more than shape versus animal. Will not approve a sad or drooping fox when a child misses a day.
- **grade-head-g2:** fennec, Eko. A 7-year-old needs a concrete animal to hold onto, and no child thinks a fox is a classmate. Kiro is a common nickname for Kyrillos; Nudj is unreadable at grade 2.
- **grade-head-g8:** stylised fennec, not too cute, Eko. Strongly against Homie: cringe at 13 to 14 and claims friendship.
- **course-designer-bilingual:** cartoon fennec, Eko (إيكو, echo, écho) is clean in all three languages. Homie loses its h in French; Nudj reads "Nudg" in Arabic script; Kiro reads as a real boy's name.
- **product-manager:** stylised fennec, Eko. Homie is too generic to own in search. Risk: Eko sits near Amazon Echo; trademark unchecked.

## Challenges (round 2)
- **course-designer-chief** made the strongest abstract case (a creature with a clear face is concrete enough and avoids the "sly fox" baggage), then conceded on condition that a child test gate is run.
- **grade-head-g8:** teens drop babyish things, not animals. Grade 8 style rules: leaner face, smaller eyes, no blush or sparkles, calm posture, no clothes or age markers, ears as the only big feature, muted orange, a listening nod instead of celebrations, an even dry-warm voice with no baby talk, no sly expressions.
- **product-manager (devil's advocate for Homie):** Homie saves marketing money because name equals brand, but the name says "I'm your buddy" in its first word, which breaks the ethics rule, and it cannot be owned in search. Keep the link with "Eko from HOMEWORK" and a house shape in the logo.

## Decision
The council recommends a stylised fennec named **Eko** (إيكو). Not a fully abstract character; not Homie, Kiro or Nudj. One species for every grade band with no human ages, so the "about 17" sibling is retired; maturity is shown by size, posture and voice. This is advice: the founder has final say on brand, but the ethics and age-fit limits hold whatever name is chosen.

## Dissent
Dr. Adel Barakat (course-designer-chief) backed an abstract character and withdrew on condition that the test gate below is run.
